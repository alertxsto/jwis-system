# JWIS Pilot — Stakeholder Interview Protocol

> Purpose: capture real DLH workflow, authority, data availability, and target
> KPIs so JWIS is validated against operational reality, not assumptions.
> **No names, quotes, GPS logs, or screenshots are stored without written consent.**

## Who to interview (minimum 3)

1. **DLH / UPST operator** — landfill/weighbridge and dispatch reality.
2. **Fleet supervisor or driver** — routes, deviations, field constraints.
3. **Waste-management domain expert** — validity of assumptions and KPIs.

## Consent (ask first, record the answer)

- May we store your role (not name) and anonymized answers for this project? (Y/N)
- May we quote you? If yes, attributed or anonymous?
- May we receive anonymized operational samples (GPS, weighbridge logs)?

## Questions — current workflow

1. How are trucks currently dispatched and monitored today?
2. Who has authority to approve a dispatch or reroute?
3. How is a route deviation noticed today, and what is the tolerance for false alerts?
4. What causes queues at Bantargebang, and what is an acceptable wait (SLA)?
5. What waste-volume information do you have per area, and at what resolution?
6. What decisions would a reliable weekly/monthly hotspot forecast change?

## Questions — data availability

- GPS/AVL feed + assigned routes? Format, frequency, access?
- Weighbridge arrival/service/departure timestamps?
- Vehicle status (active/damaged/maintenance)?
- Event permits with crowd estimates?
- Shift rules and truck capacities?

## Questions — targets

- What KPI improvement would make JWIS worth adopting?
- What would make you distrust the system?

## After each interview

- Record which product assumption was confirmed or changed.
- Log findings in `VALIDATION_LOG.md`.
