# Five-Day Semifinal Video Production Plan

## 12 July — Lock Story and Identity

- Fill team name, member names, and roles in `cards.html` and the submission checklist.
- Read the English script aloud and replace words that are difficult to pronounce.
- Confirm the selected case provider naming with the registration entry.

## 13 July — Stabilize the Demo

- Run all backend tests and frontend build.
- Rehearse the dispatch flow five times.
- Test assistant, MapLibre, Open-Meteo, OSRM, PDF export, and Field App.
- Record one rough screen-only rehearsal and time every section.

## 14 July — Record Main Takes

- Record opening/closing cards in 1080p.
- Record two complete screen-demo takes.
- Record human English narration in a quiet room.

## 15 July — Edit and Review

- Edit to 08:15-09:00.
- Add readable captions for metrics and section titles.
- Check that required logos and team identity are visible.
- Export H.264/AAC MP4 at 1080p.

## 16 July — Upload and Access Test

- Watch the exported MP4 from beginning to end.
- Upload to Google Drive or YouTube.
- Test the link in incognito mode and on a second device.
- Keep one backup link.

## 17 July — Submit Early

- Submit through the competition website using the registered account.
- Save a screenshot of the successful submission page.
- Do not wait until the final hour.
